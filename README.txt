LANGUAGE EDITIONS & STRUCTURE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Language Selection Opener:
   - When visitors open the invitation, they are welcomed by an elegant opener featuring KCC & PPA crests and an animated cardiac pulse.
   - Visitors choose from 3 language cards:
     * 🇬🇧 English -> index.html
     * 🇱🇰 தமிழ் (Tamil) -> ta.html
     * 🇱🇰 සිංහල (Sinhala) -> si.html
   - Ambient heartbeat audio plays upon language selection (following modern mobile audio policies).

2. Top-Bar Instant Language Switcher:
   - Every page features an instant 3-way language switcher in the header:
     [EN] | [தமிழ்] | [සිංහල]
   - Allows users to switch languages at any point during their visit.

3. Conversational Sri Lankan Language & Practical English Terms:
   - Carefully adapted to everyday Sri Lankan speech (Kandy/Gelioya context) by blending clear native wording with universally understood English terms:
     * English terms used naturally: Blood Donation Camp, Donor, Register, Date, Time, Venue, Full Name, Contact Number, WhatsApp Number, NIC, Driving License, Tattoo, Antibiotics, 4 Months Gap, Empty stomach, Donor Pass.
     * Avoids overly formal or archaic dictionary vocabulary, making the invitations and registration steps effortless for youths, past pupils, and the general public.

4. Native Mobile Form & WhatsApp Integration:
   - Registration messages sent to Mr. M.S.M. Ramjan (+94 77 513 8838) are automatically formatted in the user's chosen language:
     * English WhatsApp format on index.html
     * Tamil WhatsApp format on ta.html
     * Sinhala WhatsApp format on si.html
   - Digital Donor Passes are also generated in the respective language.
   - Direct Google Maps link (https://maps.app.goo.gl/GUvWcV5GCEfPuANm7) is included across all pages, passes, and calendar invitations.

5. In-Built Data Collection & Coordinator Admin Portal (admin.html):
   - Every registration is automatically collected and recorded, whether the donor registers via WhatsApp or clicks "Get Donor Pass" directly.
   - Access the Admin Portal via `admin.html` or the discreet "🔒 Coordinator Portal" link in the footer.
   - Protected by Passcode (Default: `kcc2026`).
   - Admin Features:
     * Live KPI counters: Total Donors, Male/Female split, Blood Group breakdown (A+, B+, O+, AB+), and Time Slots.
     * Instant search & filtering by donor name, phone, blood group, or slot.
     * 1-tap Direct Call (📞) and 1-tap WhatsApp chat (💬) for each donor.
     * 1-click Export to Microsoft Excel / CSV with UTF-8 BOM (preserves Sinhala & Tamil names).
     * 1-click Print Master Attendance Roster for the reception desk on Dec 06.
     * Manual Add Donor modal for walk-in or phone call donors.

6. Google Sheets Cloud Webhook (google-apps-script.js):
   - For GitHub Pages static hosting, donor submissions can automatically land directly in your private Google Sheet in real-time.
   - See `google-apps-script.js` for quick 2-minute setup instructions.
