Health Guidelines

Who Can Give Blood?

Please check these simple points before participating:

✓

Age

18 to 55 Years
(Regular healthy donors accepted up to 60)

✓

Weight

Minimum 50 kg (110 lbs)
Ensures complete safety for you

✓

General Health

Feeling healthy & fit
No active cold, fever, or infectious illness

✓

Donation Gap

At least 4 Months
Since your previous whole blood donation

✓

Before Coming

Good sleep (6–8 hrs)
Eat a meal & drink water (do not come hungry)

✓

What to Bring

Original Photo ID
National Identity Card (NIC) or Driving License

⚠️ Please wait and reschedule if: You had a tattoo or body piercing within the last 6 months, are currently taking antibiotics, or are pregnant / breastfeeding.
